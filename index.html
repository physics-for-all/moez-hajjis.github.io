01 — ABOUT ME

Research driven by materials.

I am a materials science researcher holding a PhD in Physics from the University of Tunis El Manar, Tunisia, in cotutelle with the University of Santiago de Compostela, Spain.

My research focuses on the synthesis, structural and physical characterization, and development of functional materials for optoelectronic, energy and environmental applications. My interests include metal oxides, perovskites, MXenes, semiconductor heterostructures, photocatalysis and emerging functional materials.

I combine experimental materials science, advanced characterization and computational approaches to investigate structure–property relationships and improve material performance.

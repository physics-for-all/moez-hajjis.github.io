01 — PROFILE

Bridging lab physics and industry.

Ph.D. in Physics (2025, international cotutelle between the University of Tunis El Manar and the Universidade de Santiago de Compostela, Spain). I specialize in perovskite and metal-oxide thin-film solar cells.

My research covers the full device chain: chemical spray pyrolysis of CuO-ZnO, TiO2-CuO, and CuO-SnO2-F couples; FAI additive engineering of CsPbI perovskite films; structural, optical, and electrochemical characterization; and multi-junction device modeling using SCAPS-1D and Silvaco.

A central focus of my work is solar cell stabilization and lifetime extension — analyzing degradation mechanisms in perovskite absorbers under ambient air, developing encapsulation strategies (glass-glass architectures, edge sealing, UV-filtering, moisture-barrier approaches), interface/defect passivation, and accelerated ageing / ISO-type stability testing.

This expertise is consolidated in 20 peer-reviewed Q1-Q2 articles (9 as first author), an h-index of 8, and 159 citations. It is complemented by industrial internships in PV system design and installation with Olfier, Soltec Ingenieros, and Iberdrola España.

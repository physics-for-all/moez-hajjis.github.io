01 — PROFESSIONAL PROFILE

Bridging laboratory physics and real-world deployment.

Ph.D. in Physics (2025, international "cotutelle" between the University of Tunis El Manar and the Universidade de Santiago de Compostela, Spain), specialist in perovskite and metal-oxide thin-film solar cells.

My research covers the full device chain: synthesis of thin films and ceramics — deposition of oxide and perovskite absorbers and transport layers (chemical spray pyrolysis of CuO-ZnO, TiO2-CuO and CuO-SnO2-F couples; formamidinium-iodide (FAI) additive engineering of CsPbI perovskite films), their structural, optical and electrochemical characterization, and multi-junction device modeling (SCAPS-1D, Silvaco).

A central focus of my work is solar cell stabilization and lifetime extension — analysis of degradation mechanisms in perovskite absorbers under ambient air, encapsulation strategies (glass-glass architectures, edge sealing, UV-filtering and moisture-barrier approaches), interface and defect passivation, and accelerated ageing / ISO-type stability testing.

This expertise is consolidated in 20 peer-reviewed Q1-Q2 articles (9 as first author), h-index 8, and 159 citations. It is complemented by industrial internships in the design, sizing and installation of photovoltaic systems with Olffer, Soltec Ingenieros, and Iberdrola España in Galicia (Spain), bridging laboratory-scale device physics with real-world PV deployment for industry.
